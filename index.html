Welcome to The Swaha Signatures

In the heart of Berawa, The Swaha Signatures was created with one simple vision: a place that feels like home but elevated. A tropical lifestyle hotel where contemporary design meets personalized hospitality. A TASTE OF BERAWA

Dining Experiences

Enjoy delicious flavors and relaxed dining moments inspired by the spirit of Bali.

Restaurant

A contemporary all-day dining destination offering a delightful fusion of locally inspired and international flavours in a contemporary tropical setting. Start the day with a semi à la carte breakfast, where made-to-order signature dishes are complemented by a carefully curated buffet selection.

Crux

Inspired by the Southern Cross (Crux) constellation, a timeless guide for travelers, Crux is the social heart of The Swaha Signatures. Here, handcrafted cocktails, breathtaking sunsets, and meaningful connections come together to create unforgettable evenings above the vibrant energy of Berawa.
